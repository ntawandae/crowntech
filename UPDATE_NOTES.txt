EMAIL ON RENDER IN ONE LINE
Render's free plan blocks SMTP ports 25/465/587, so use MAIL_PORT=80, MAIL_USE_SSL=false, MAIL_USE_TLS=true,
plus BEHIND_PROXY=true and PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://crowntech-55z4.onrender.com. Full table in the guide.
